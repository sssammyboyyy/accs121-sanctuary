# ACCS121 Test 2B Cram Engine — Chapters 12 & 16

Interactive, memo-grounded speed-learning engine for North-West University (NWU) ACCS121 / ACFS121 Test 2B.

🔗 **Live App:** [https://sssammyboyyy.github.io/accs121-sanctuary/](https://sssammyboyyy.github.io/accs121-sanctuary/)

---

### Features & 5-Pillar Architecture

1. **🧭 Pillar 01: Zero-to-One Map** — Core data routing pipeline, the 2.0 free layout/communication marks, and statement classification.
2. **🧮 Pillar 02: 7 Bracket Workings Drill** — Dual modes:
   - **Official Memo Baseline:** 2024–2025 NWU Spitz Traders memorandum values (0.5 marks per raw number in brackets + 0.5P method marks).
   - **🎲 Random Calculator Drill:** Spin fresh numbers, punch them into your physical non-programmable calculator, and verify accuracy with the instant check validator.
3. **📋 Pillar 03: Statement & Note Templates** — Dynamic Statement of Profit or Loss and Trade & Other Receivables Note that recompute live with your active numbers.
4. **🏦 Pillar 04: Bank Recon Sorter Game (Chapter 16)** — Master the mirror rule between Cash Journals (CRJ/CPJ) and Bank Reconciliation Statement (Cr/Dr).
5. **🎯 Pillar 05: Exam-Room Checklist & Time Discipline** — Strategic 100-minute time-boxing (strict 2.0 min/mark).
